## UX review (mobile first)

From screenshots at 360 and 390 px (light and dark), German, and desktop. Items 1–8 and 10 of that review are in 2.20.

1. **Birth date under the child's name** at the top of the result.
2. **Growth: chart first,** with the "Dodaj mjerenje" form folded away.
3. **Measurements as a small table** (date, weight, length, head, percentiles).
4. **A one-line intro on the first visit.**
5. **Desktop: form and results side by side.**
6. **Installed app on iPhone: safe-area insets** for the notch and home bar.

The pregnancy week-by-week idea was dropped on purpose: the app calculates from the user's own dates and doesn't
host medical content that would need sourcing, review and maintenance in four languages.
